We got ourselves a good one today.

Phil Collins' "No Jacket Required" is an absolute classic, in case you weren't tipped off by the massive sticker on the shrinkwrap.

If you're keeping up with the Commodore "What's stuck in my head" updates, you'll probably remember "One More Night" from May 30th, 2026, so I won't bother re-explaining my relationship with that song. I will mention that "Sussudio" never fails to remind me of Hal leading the revolution.

I have to say I was quite pleased to find this, at a very reasonable price no less. The lady running the till was equally impressed, so I fear this doesn't happen very often. Either way, quite a good haul.
