Re-Volt Xbox is my modification of the Xbox port of Acclaim's Re-Volt, with the ultimate goal being to finish their job.

Download the Latest Build (2.0)

2.0 is much improved compared to earlier builds, and fixes many bugs and issues.

IMPORTANT: When updating from a pre-2.0 build, make sure to copy all the files, not just the XBE.

Changes include:

  • Tracks will now play their appropriate music

  • Re-enabled Garden Battle

  • Improvements to DLC cars (including the ability to use them online)

  • Support for custom DLC levels

  • Added the stars back to Stunt Arena

  • Enabled Clockwork Carnage

  • Enabled loading of Time Trial ghosts

  • Enabled the podium cutscene at the end of championship

  • Added rumble support

  • Lots and lots of smaller bug fixes
