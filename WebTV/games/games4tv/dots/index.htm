Dotty!
Warning! Addictive Game Ahead!

Dotty presents you with a grid of colored dots. You can collect any dots that have another of the same color next to it, either in the same row or the same column. For example, in picture here, a click on any of the bright red dots will collect all eight of them!

The more dots you collect in a single click, the higher your score. (You can collect two dots, but you don't get any points for it.) When the dots are collected, the dots above them will fall down to fill their places. When an entire column is collected, the remaining columns will move together to fill up the space. If you collect all of the dots, you'll get a bonus and advance to the next level! But leave any left over, and they'll be deducted from your score.

As you advance, you'll get more and more colors to clear, and the game gets harder. It will drive you dotty trying to become a master!
