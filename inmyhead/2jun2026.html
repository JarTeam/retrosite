I Just Threw Out The Love Of My Dreams

weezer

1996

I am not proud of this one.

I've never considered myself a fan of "emo teenager music" (as I like to call it), or weezer at all.

However, YouTube ended up recommending me a 17 year old animation featuring the song. You know how it goes, when YouTube recommends a 17 year old video you have to click on it.

The song was very catchy to me, to the point that I was forced to put my bias aside.
